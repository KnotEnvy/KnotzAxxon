# Bot execution speed and limits

The revised bulk runner advances up to36,000 synthetic frames in one browser call,
with a render interval of36,000 plus current-state checkpoint/terminal draws. It
defers HUD drawing to the checkpoint and holds the requested resolution. Every
original Engine update/late hook, input fold, collision, weapon, resource and
hit-stop calculation still runs. Audio uses the compiled game's unavailable-audio
branch; these runs do not test sound. Realtime Terra Medium plays remain separate,
with actual rAF timestamps and every frame drawn. The current observed requirement
is30 plays, five per loop band.

This implementation **does not meet the requested couple-minute1000-run target**.
No remaining900 batch or revised final campaign was launched. The old RC02 batch
retains100 valid audited production episodes and its immutable identity. A revised
QA recipe requires a new freeze before any new final campaign is counted.

## Reproducibility controls and scope

Early natural comparisons did not give exact replay parity. Investigation found:

- Seeding in a browser call before stopping rAF allowed another frame to consume
  shared random values. Stop/seed/start are now atomic.
- Subtracting a frame duration from a changing wall timestamp could cross Engine's
  1/60 substep boundary through rounding. The test clock now supplies an exact delta.
- Streaming builds one or two chunks according to measured build time; ambient
  effects share RNG with combat. Audio detune is also shared but wall-time throttled.
- Pickup bob phase uses native `Math.random`; renderer UUID creation also consumes it.

The successful comparison therefore selects the existing one-chunk streaming branch,
seeds native randomness separately per call site and runs both arms with audio
unavailable. These are **diagnostic controls only**. Normal bulk runs keep the
original timed streaming and native randomness. Exact parity is established under
those controlled conditions, not as a claim of exact natural replay at every speed.
Every comparison result is marked `comparisonFixture:true`; credit audit rejects it
even if someone changes its kind to final. No controlled comparison earns campaign,
player, listening, sustained-FPS or physical-device credit.

The CPU regressions additionally execute the actual Engine._loop through both
runner modes, proving equal simulation/late/input counts, retained hit stop and
damage, reduced draw/HUD calls, owner restoration and failure guards.

Further progress toward a two-minute campaign requires a materially faster logic
execution path and new parity evidence. Increasing dt, shortening episodes, granting
resources or replacing collisions would change the coverage and cannot be presented
as the completed full-game campaign.


## Current-recipe natural pilot

`pilot-speed-natural-09` exercised the current recipe against the exact production
package without the comparison's native-RNG or streaming controls. The six opening
matrix rows completed genuine outcomes (three victories and three deaths), normal
starting resources, two draws each and no errors. Its before/after source, entire
harness and package were stable; the coordinator exited0 and released its GPU lease.
The actual per-seed times and hashes are retained in
[BOT-SPEED-NATURAL.json](BOT-SPEED-NATURAL.json). These are uncredited Low/Classic
synthetic-analog pilots, not the full matrix or an exact natural replay comparison.

All342 CPU regressions pass, including five new scheduling/ownership/admission
checks. The30-row player-plan, explicit-note and pipeline CPU checks also pass.
No production source or assets changed, no fresh candidate was frozen, and no
remaining900/fresh1000 campaign, player episode, judge round or publication ran.
