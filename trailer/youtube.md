## Description

Match altitude. Read your shadow. Break the fortress.

KNOTZAXXON is a 3D isometric rail shooter that plays free in your browser. One interceptor, eight hostile sectors and the Iron Sentinel waiting at the core. Fly it from the classic isometric angle with the CRT overlay switched on, or switch to the modern or chase camera. It's the same fortress either way.

▶ Play now: https://knotenvy.github.io/KnotzAxxon/
Keyboard, gamepad or touch. No install, no account.

What's in it:
• Altitude is the whole game. Your shadow, the drop-line and the altimeter's amber band tell you which gap in the wall is yours.
• Three camera rigs from the SYSTEMS menu: CLASSIC 45 (true isometric, with an optional CRT overlay), MODERN 20 and CHASE.
• Eight sectors: the outer fortress, flak batteries, open-space fighter waves, reactor coolant gates, the inner citadel and the Gauntlet.
• The Iron Sentinel: shoot off its shoulder cannons, punish the open reactor, and land six hits on the charging rack to blow its missile in its hand.
• Style scoring for threads, low passes and grazes, chains up to x8, and S/A/B/C sector grades.
• A daily sortie (one seeded fortress per day, the same for everyone) and shareable six-character fortress codes.
• Every texture, model and sound is generated in code. There are no asset files.

Everything in this trailer is real gameplay, filmed frame by frame from the game itself and scored on the game's own synthesizer.

KNOTZAXXON is an independent project inspired by classic isometric arcade shooters. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any other game or publisher.
