DOCTRINE

            
    
              
  • Guns fire level. Match the target lane and altitude. Flight Assist enables aim steering.
    • 
              
  • Altitude is everything. Your shadow on the deck tells you where you really are. Read it.
    • 
              
  • Walls have gaps. The altimeter paints the safe band in amber. Thread it.
    • 
              
  • Fuel cells: shoot them at matching altitude to refuel. Ramming them damages your ship.
    • 
              
  • Altitude echo: the narrow band beside your altimeter shows the nearest visible target in your firing lane. Green means matching height; amber stripes show wall clearance.
    • 
              
  • Fight in view. Off-screen targets cannot take player-shot damage. Keep enemies in the picture to confirm your kills.
    • 
              
  • Chain kills within three seconds to build your multiplier up to x8. The timer turns red before your chain expires.
    • 
              
  • Style pays. Thread a gap close to its edge, dive low under arches and graze enemy fire for bonus points that keep your chain alive.
    • 
              
  • Sector grades rate each sector on targets destroyed and damage taken. A clean sector earns a bonus; 50,000 and 150,000 points earn extra hull.
    • 
              
  • Daily sortie: one fortress per day, the same for every pilot. Beat the Sentinel to continue into a harder loop.
    • 
              
  • Radar towers are worth a fortune and blind enemy fire prediction.
    • 
              
  • The roll grants brief invulnerability and vents weapon heat.
